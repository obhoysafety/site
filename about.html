About OBHOY

A Safer Tomorrow Starts With What We Do Today.

OBHOY is conceived as a dedicated Child and Women's Safety Initiative—not a generic safety organization.

Its central purpose is to strengthen prevention, awareness, dignity, responsible support pathways and community participation around child protection and women's safety.

Our Core Focus

  • Child protection and child abuse prevention
  • Women's and girls' safety
  • Violence and harassment prevention
  • Digital safety and cyber abuse awareness
  • Rights, awareness and safe reporting
  • Support and responsible referral

Safety + Dignity + Protection + Courage + Hope + Trust

These values guide the intended emotional and practical experience of OBHOY.

Bangladesh Focus — Women and children are at the centre of our awareness, safeguarding and support approach.
