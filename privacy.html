Privacy & Safeguarding

Privacy and safeguarding are central to responsible work with women, children and vulnerable communities.

Privacy & Data Protection Principles

This page is a prototype framework, not a completed legal policy.

  • Explain what information may be collected.
  • Explain why information is collected and how it is protected.
  • Explain when information may need to be shared.
  • Describe anonymous and confidential options accurately.
  • Address children's privacy and safeguarding limitations.
  • Do not promise absolute confidentiality or 100% security.
A qualified legal, privacy and safeguarding review is required before public launch.

Safeguarding & Reporting Principles

OBHOY should use careful, responsible procedures and should not imply emergency, police, hospital or government authority unless verified.
