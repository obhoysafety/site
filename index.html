Why OBHOY exists

Every Child Deserves Safety. Every Woman Deserves Dignity.

Violence can be physical, psychological, sexual, domestic, social, or digital. Many people do not know where to turn, are afraid to speak, or are unsure which service can help. OBHOY exists to support prevention, awareness, protection and responsible referral.

01 — Child Safety

Protecting Childhood. Preventing Harm.

Child abuse prevention, personal safety, online safety, bullying prevention, child rights, safer schools and responsible reporting.

The OBHOY Approach

Prevent. Protect. Empower.

◉

Prevent

Prevention begins with knowledge through awareness campaigns, education, school initiatives and digital-safety learning.

⌂

Protect

When someone is at risk, knowing where to turn matters. OBHOY is designed to help identify appropriate support pathways.

✦

Empower

People are safer when they understand their rights and have confidence and resources to seek help.
